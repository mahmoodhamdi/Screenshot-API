### 3. Webhook Documentation

```markdown
## Webhook Verification

All webhooks are signed with HMAC-SHA256. Verify using:

1. Get `X-Webhook-Timestamp` and `X-Webhook-Signature` headers
2. Construct payload: `${timestamp}.${body}`
3. Compute HMAC-SHA256 with your webhook secret
4. Compare with signature (timing-safe)

### Retry Policy
- 5 attempts with exponential backoff
- Delays: ~1s, ~2s, ~4s, ~8s, ~16s
- Random jitter added to prevent thundering herd
```
